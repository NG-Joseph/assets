DATA BUNDLE ACTIVATION REQ

From the home page, click on "Buy Data". select the bundle category "Data Bundles". Select the bundle type under daily, weekly, monthly, 2months, 3months, 6months, yearly. Select the payment method: Pay with Airtime: select your number to complete the transaction Pay with MoMo PSB: click "Send OTP", input the OTP once received. You will be asked to enter MoMo pin to complete the transaction. Ensure that the line is registered on MoMo. Pay with Bank Transfer: You will be given an account details to transfer the amount to within 46 minutes. Once you have transferred the amount, click on "I've sent the money". Pay with Card: input your card details to proceed. An OTP will be sent. Enter the OTP to complete the transaction. pay with USSD: Click on the drop down and select your bank if listed. Then click on the Dial button.

DATA BUNDLE STOP AUTORENEWAL

From the home page, click on "More" the 3 dot icon at the bottom right of the app. Under "My Account" click on "Manage Subscription" Under "Active Subscription",you will see he active bundle details. To Activate: Dial *305#, select option 2 for "Discover new services" Select "2CanPlay" or the service you desire. Select the validity you desire (Daily or Weekly). You will receive a prompt to Accept or Cancel to complete the activation. Click "Accept Auto-renewal" or "Accept One-Off" to proceed. An activation message will be sent via SMS.
